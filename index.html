How it works

Four Steps, About a Week.

1

Screening Call

A short call to confirm your data can support a review, and to agree scope and price. If it can’t, you pay nothing.

2

Send Your Exports

Two finalized, comparable periods, plus recipes for the items you name. CSV or XLSX preferred.

3

Review and Memo

Findings, a calculation appendix with source rows, and a verification checklist. Delivered within five business days of complete inputs.

4

Walkthrough

A 20-minute call with your manager to go through what to verify first.

What’s Included

A findings memo, an evidence appendix, a verification checklist, and a clear statement of what the evidence does and doesn’t support. Scope is set on the screening call: up to three nominated variances and two batch recipes per location is the standard review.

What’s Not Included

On-site counts, live system changes, recipe or inventory setup, bookkeeping or tax advice, fraud findings, and savings guarantees.

Questions

Before You Book.

Two finalized, comparable periods of inventory and sales exports (CSV or XLSX), plus recipes for the items you name. No staff names or payroll.

Do You Need Access to My Systems?

No. I work from exports only, and nothing is changed in your POS or inventory system.

What If My Data Can’t Support a Review?

I’ll say so on the screening call, and you pay nothing.

Will You Tell Me Who Is Responsible?

No. The memo never points at staff, and fraud findings are not part of the review. It shows what the data supports and what to verify first.

How Is My Data Handled?

It is used only for your review and deleted after delivery unless you ask me to keep it.

Can I Start With One Venue?

Yes. Groups are priced per venue after the screening call, and the review can be run across all of them side by side.

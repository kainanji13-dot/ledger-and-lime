How it works

About a Week, Start to Finish.

You send exports you already have. I do the analysis. You get a short list of what to fix and what to check.

1

Screening Call

15 minutes

Which system do you export from, which variance do you keep chasing, and can the data support a review? If not, you pay nothing.

2

You Send Exports

Day 0

Two finalized, comparable periods: inventory counts and item-level sales, plus recipes for the items we name.

3

I Review

Up to 5 business days

I run the numbers, check every finding by hand and separate recording issues from things worth a physical check.

4

Memo and Checklist

Day 5

A plain-language findings memo, an evidence appendix with source rows, and a verification checklist.

5

Walkthrough

20 minutes

A call with your manager to go through what to verify first. You decide what to act on.

What to Send

  • Inventory counts for two finalized, comparable periods
  • Item-level sales for the same periods (pours or units sold)
  • Recipes for any batches or cocktails you want checked
  • Purchases or received orders, if available
  • Waste, comp and spill logs, if you keep them

CSV or Excel is ideal. If your system can export it, I can work with it. No staff names or payroll.

What You Get

  • A findings memo: recording issues, physical checks, and count noise
  • Dollar value on every finding
  • An evidence appendix with the source rows
  • A verification checklist for your manager
  • A clear statement of what the data can’t tell us

Your Data

Used only for your review and deleted after delivery unless you ask me to keep it. I never need access to your systems.

Price

Fixed, per venue, quoted after the screening call. If the data can’t support a review, you pay nothing. If the memo doesn’t give you a specific, checkable next step, I refund the fee.

Not Included

On-site counts, live system changes, recipe or inventory setup, bookkeeping or tax advice, fraud findings, and savings guarantees.

Want This for Your Venue?

Tell me which variance your team keeps investigating and which system you export from. I’ll reply to set up a short screening call.
